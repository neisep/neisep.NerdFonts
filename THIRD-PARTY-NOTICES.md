## 6. Known upstream licensing uncertainties

These questions come from the upstream projects and cannot be settled by this package alone. They are listed so that you can make your own assessment. They are not legal advice.

**Release 1.0.1 decision:** the package maintainer has reviewed these uncertainties and chosen to rely on Nerd Fonts v3.5.1 and the current upstream license grants, including for Pomicons, the combined font's OFL portions, and brand icons. This is an acceptance of the documented upstream licensing risks, not independent confirmation of relicensing authority or third-party logo permissions. The font, upstream notices, and license texts are preserved unmodified.

1. **Logos and brand icons.** Font Logos, Devicons, Font Awesome brand icons, some Material Design Icons, Nerd Fonts' custom icons and Octicons' GitHub marks depict third-party logos. Upstream licensed the drawings, but the logo owners may hold separate rights. Open upstream issue: https://github.com/lukas-w/font-logos/issues/123
2. **OFL portions.** Nerd Fonts labels the Symbols Only font as MIT, but it contains Pomicons and Weather Icons glyphs, which the SIL OFL 1.1 requires to remain under the OFL. This package does not relabel anything: those glyphs remain under the OFL 1.1. How OFL condition 5 applies to the combined font remains an upstream licensing uncertainty; including the license texts does not settle it.
3. **Pomicons:** the project was licensed under CC BY-NC-ND 3.0 until January 2022, when the license file was changed to the SIL OFL 1.1 with Gabriele Lana as copyright holder. The font metadata names Davide Bignotti as copyright holder ("All rights reserved") and trademark owner. Whether the OFL grant covers the designer's rights cannot be determined from public sources. The trademark notice is also not carried in the bundled font's metadata. The history and both sets of notices are reproduced [above](#pomicons).
4. **Weather Icons:** no completed copyright line exists upstream.
5. **Material Design Icons:** the icons under "their respective licenses" are not identified upstream.
6. **Font Awesome:** whether CC BY 4.0 (icons) or SIL OFL 1.1 (font files) governs glyphs rebuilt from SVG into another font. Both licenses are included. The license file Nerd Fonts ships predates the bundled 6.5.1 icons; the notices that accompany those icons are reproduced [above](#font-awesome-free).
7. **Hack:** whether any bundled glyph descends from Bitstream Vera. That license is included.

## 7. If you ship an app that uses this package

If your app distributes the font, for example by calling `NerdFont.ExtractTo` or by shipping `neisep.NerdFonts.dll`, it redistributes the third-party material above. Include this file and the `licenses/` folder with your app (or make them otherwise available to your users). Both are in the NuGet package, e.g. `~/.nuget/packages/neisep.nerdfonts/<version>/`.
